Privacy Policy

Giant Panda Bamboo Dash (熊猫竹林冲刺) — last updated 2026-11-19

Short version: this game collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no network requests, no permissions. Your progress and best scores stay on your own device.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal data.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Which groves you have unlocked, your best score per grove, and your language choice.
  • This lives in the app's private storage. It is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also clear it from the system's app storage settings at any time.

3. Permissions

The app requests no system permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, notifications, contacts or the internet.

4. Third parties

No third-party SDKs, ad networks, analytics or crash-reporting services are included. The game ships entirely inside the installation package and loads no remote content.

5. Children

The game contains no ads, no purchases, no chat and no user-generated content, and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes

If a future version adds advertising, purchases or online features, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the store listing will say so.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or this app: adsl008008@qq.com

什么都不收集。游戏不会获取姓名、邮箱、手机号、位置、通讯录、相册、设备标识符 或任何其他个人信息。
